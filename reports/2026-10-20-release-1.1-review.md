### H-1 — /claim fetches a route that does not exist (High, fixed)
- **Impact:** No allocation renders for anyone; every beneficiary sees "We could not load your allocation."
  Refreshing after a claim fails too, so `trackClaim` polls for 20 minutes and then gives up.
- **Root cause:** `apps/webapp/src/features/claim/client/api.ts:51` requests `/api/vesting`. The route is
  `app/api/claim/vesting/route.ts`, and there is no rewrite. The bug came in with `d81a1fe`, the consolidation of
  16 October.
- **PoC:** `curl https://mintabear-app.mintstage.io/api/vesting?chainId=46630&account=0x…` → 404. New test
  `tests/claim-api-routes.test.ts` failed on `e3c608b` and passes with the fix.
- **Fix:** `e610af6` on `mnt/defect-claim-vesting-path` (off `feat/contracts` = `e3c608b`). Webapp check: 90/90
  pass, typecheck clean.

### H-2 — Worker crash from one request (High)
- **Impact:**
  - One unauthenticated request ends the process. Relays, outcomes, tickets and payout records all stop.
  - After 10 restarts Railway gives up.
  - Winners can lose prizes, because claims close one hour after `end`.
- **Root cause:** `apps/box-worker/src/health.ts:43` `new URL(request.url …)` runs outside any try/catch.
  `railway.json` sets `restartPolicyMaxRetries: 10`.
- **PoC:** `printf 'GET http://[ HTTP/1.1\r\nHost: x\r\n\r\n' | nc <worker> 8080`.
- **Fix:**
  - Guard the handler and answer 400.
  - Log `uncaughtException`.
  - Lift the restart cap.
  - Serve tickets through the webapp, not a public worker domain.

### M-1 — Stale quote executed (Medium)
- **Root cause:** `SwapForm.tsx:81` keeps `phase: 'quoted'` while a new quote loads, and `swap()` (`:112`) executes
  `phase.quote`, while the label shows the current inputs.
- **Fix:** Key the quote by `amount|from|to|address`, and enable Swap only when that key matches the current inputs.

### M-2 — `minBlock` cache eviction (Medium)
- **Root cause:** `vesting.ts:152,161-163`. A `minBlock` in the future deletes the shared snapshot, re-reads every
  wallet, then deletes it again.
- **Fix:**
  - Refuse a `minBlock` above the head plus a margin.
  - Never delete the shared entry on a request's behalf; answer retryable instead.

### M-3 — One rate-limit bucket by default (Medium)
- **Root cause:** `packages/server-kit/src/rate-limit.ts:43` returns `'shared'` at 0 hops. The `.env.example` value
  is 0, and DEPLOY.md says 1, but nothing enforces it.
- **Fix:**
  - Refuse 0 in production config.
  - Confirm the Railway value on staging and production.

### M-4 — Mixed chains (Medium)
- **Root cause:**
  - `layout.tsx:28` hard-codes 4663 for the wallet, and `WalletButton` asks each new address to switch to it.
  - Staking defaults to 4663 (`mntd/server/config.ts:25`), and `StakePanel.tsx:107` switches the wallet to 4663 on
    its own.
- **Impact:** On staging, a tester can lock real MNTD for up to 365 days. On /claim, the nav pushes the user off the
  claim's chain.
- **Fix:**
  - Set `STAKING_CHAIN_ID=46630` on staging (env).
  - Make the nav's chain follow the page.
  - Show a testnet banner.
- No transaction can be signed on the wrong chain. `ClaimPanel` gates on the chain, `writeContract` passes
  `chainId`, and the server refuses a foreign chain.

### M-5…M-7, L-3, L-4, L-10 — the worker's resilience
Detail is in the review notes:
- `worker.ts:102-120` rethrows into `main.ts:82-89`, which skips the payout step.
- `chains.ts:14-20` and `main.ts:27-45`: startup, and the alarms that start after it.
- `config.ts:18`: the deploy-block default.
- `worker.ts:188-204` and `vault-claims.ts:185`: the scan cursors.
- `main.ts:87-88`: error messages.

Fix as one hardening branch of the worker (ours):
- independent stages, each with its own try/catch and alarm;
- retry with backoff at startup;
- required deploy blocks;
- a confirmation lag or overlap on log scans;
- redacted error text;
- a reverted relay re-checked against `nextToResolve`.

## Sound (checked)
- **Whitelist register:** row-locked pool, then account; DB CHECKs and unique keys; freeze via
  `FOR SHARE`/`FOR UPDATE`; single-use 10-minute challenges bound to DID, kind, wallet and quantity;
  ES256 Privy JWT checks; service token compared in constant time; lowercase-only DB addresses; CSV caps of
  1,000/3,222/4,222 at import and freeze.
- **Claim:**
  - Prepare cannot claim for another account (`owner == account`, and `release` pays `owner()`).
  - The client signs only the call it rebuilt itself, and receipt status is checked.
  - Pending state is keyed by chain, account and wallet, with a cross-tab lock.
- **Relay proxy:** allowlisted endpoints and segments, a fixed host, `redirect: 'error'`, and the key server-only.
- **Staking:** an exact approval to the verified contract, and `STAKING_TOKEN` is checked on mainnet.
- **Headers:** CSP with nonce, HSTS preload, `frame-ancestors 'none'`, nosniff; no CORS origin echo.
- **Client:** a wallet chain check on every write; the EIP-712 domain matches `PrizeVault`; tickets only after the
  cycle starts, with the manifest and signer checked; exact burn approvals.

## Phase 2 — adversarial QA on the local stack
The stack:
- Postgres and the mock wager API;
- the whitelist API in dev auth, with a service token and `TRUST_PROXY=1`;
- the webapp as a **production build** (`next start`, `NODE_ENV=production`, hops 1, claim and staking on 46630
  over the public testnet RPC), at an integration checkout of `e3c608b` plus the five fix branches.

Every request went through the webapp's public routes. The scripts and the launcher are in
`reports/2026-10-20-phase2/`.

**Whitelist path: `whitelist.mts`, 23/23 refused or contained.**
- Auth: no bearer, a non-DID bearer and an `alg: none` token are refused.
- Routing: `..`, `%2e%2e`, unknown routes and the per-wallet lookup are not reachable through the proxy.
- Bodies: wrong method; an extra field, `quantity: 2`, `__proto__`, a body over 24 KB and malformed JSON are all
  refused before the API.
- Flow: the full bind → claim → claim flow works, and a third claim is refused.
- Wallet theft:
  - Mallory can't bind Alice's wallet, whether with his own signature or with hers.
  - Alice's nonce and signature claim nothing for him.
  - A wallet that already took wagering spots can't be bound by a second account.
- Races:
  - 8 parallel claims from one account record exactly two spots.
  - Two accounts racing for one wallet: only one gets spots.
- Rate limit (the M-3 fix, live): one visitor is limited, another is not.

**Claim, status and Relay: `claim.mts`, 29/30, and the 30th is I-5.**
- H-1 live: `/api/claim/vesting` serves the beneficiary's allocations, the old path stays 404, and a stranger gets
  an empty list.
- M-2 live: a far `minBlock` → 400 `bad_request`, 20 hostile calls in 38 ms, and a past `minBlock` is served from
  the shared snapshot.
- Inputs: wrong chain, malformed address, doubled parameter.
- Prepare: refuses a non-owner, a wallet outside the manifest, unknown fields and an oversized body. For the owner
  it returns `release` on the vesting wallet with value 0.
- Transaction status: an unknown hash is a status; trailing bytes and a huge nonce are refused; responses are
  `private`.
- Relay: path traversal and absolute-URL tricks, non-allowlisted endpoints and methods, and a 2 MB body are refused.
  An allowed read passes as JSON, and the key is never exposed.

**M-3 guard, live:** a production start with hops 0 logs `APP_TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS must be at least 1 in production`.
`/ready` answers 503, so Railway refuses the deploy and keeps the previous one.

**Not exercised here:**
- Privy-signed tokens (dev auth stands in; the JWT checks are in the API's own `adversarial.test.ts`).
- Smart-wallet signatures (`smart-wallet.test.ts`).
- Real signing on testnet: that is Phase 4.
