## For the portal team

The call surface after tranche 1 is ACT-14, COL-12 and, after tranche 2, RAF-17 in the spec.
Three constraints survive from the current build and one is new:

1. **Size every burn with `costToReach`.** Credits bank the whole amount; the part above the
   level-5 threshold is destroyed for nothing, and only the same-chain adapter can refuse it.
2. **Activation is never one click.** Route a: approve, then burn. Route b: burn on Base, then
   wait for the relayed credit; warn the holder not to move the bear meanwhile.
3. **Reads are free; poll them.** `levelOf`, `weightOf`, `linkOf`, `costToReach`, `snapshot`.
4. **There is a reset event.** `TransferNonceAdvanced` fires on every non-mint transfer, in the
   same transaction as `Transfer`. Index it as the reset.

## Decisions that should not be relitigated

- **The transfer counter, not a callback.** A callback fails open or bricks transfers; the
  counter can do neither. The client accepted it on 2026-09-15.
- **`solc 0.8.17`.** Forced by SeaDrop's exact pragma. See `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Transfer validator unset at deploy; enforcement is a runbook operation.** Limit Break V3 is
  on 4663 and OpenSea's SignedZone is an authorizer on its default list, so enforcement is
  available — but OpenSea's off-chain handling on this chain is unverified and is confirmed on
  mainnet before the switch is thrown. Never security level 5 or above. OPS-6.
- **Supply capped in code.** `MAX_BEARS` on the mint path; `maxSupply` is Studio-writable.
- **`Activation` never touches the token.** One `crediter` address is the whole route decision.
- **A credit requires `ownerOf == burner` and an unchanged counter.** An approved operator
  cannot spend an owner's $MNTD, and a burn never lands on a bear that has changed hands.
- **Passive snapshot for raffles, committed before the randomness request.** No holder
  deadlines (sequencer screening), no bear counted twice, no seed-tailored snapshot.
- **Fuzz and invariant harnesses are the internal auditor's.** The developer writes
  deterministic unit tests; the SoW's "fuzzing report" is the auditor's output.
- **Final-state prose in specs and docs.** No "was / now".

## Accepted risks

- **Slither "locked ether"** on `Activation`: Solady marks ownership functions `payable`; only
  the owner could lock their own ETH by attaching value. Accepted.
- **Event ordering in the same-chain adapter**: the record is written before `burnFrom`; the
  hostile paths were traced (re-entering still needs each `burnFrom` to succeed; transferring the
  bear from inside `burnFrom` voids the caller's own record). Accepted.
- **Overshoot beyond level 5** is banked and destroyed; the portal is required to size burns
  with `costToReach`.
- **Route b trusts the attester to relay real burns.** Every credit carries the Base burn id as
  `ref`, so credits are auditable against Base events; the attester cannot credit a bear the
  burner does not own or across a transfer.
- **Raffle snapshots are supplied by MINT's worker.** They are committed before the seed exists
  and reproducible by anyone from 4663 state at the named block.
