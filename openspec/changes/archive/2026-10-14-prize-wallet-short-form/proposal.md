# Proposal

## Why

The prize wallet's short form reads `0xf6c0…e3e3` in the specification, the register and the
documents built from them. The address is `0xf6c02F0fDAC5c03EE9f1cc60A5D9875Efc4c83e3` (MINT,
28 September 2026), so its short form is `0xf6c0…83e3`. MINT noticed the mismatch in the meeting
of 12 October. The full address is right everywhere it appears.

## What Changes

- **RAF-33** and **OPS-1** carry the short form `0xf6c0…83e3`.
- The register (CQ-22) and the prose views are corrected with them.

No requirement is added or retired, and no Scenario changes.
