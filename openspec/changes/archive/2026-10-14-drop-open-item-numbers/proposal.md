# Proposal

## Why

Requirements pointed at §10's open items by number (O1…O6). The numbers follow the open
questions' dates, so every answer renumbers them and ripples into requirements. The decision each
stands for is stable.

## What Changes

- **DEL-8, DEL-10, OPS-1, OPS-5**: each open-item number becomes the decision it stands for
  (`→ CQ-n`). DEL-8 no longer waits for team-bear ids: every bear plays (CQ-20).

No requirement is added or retired, and no Scenario changes.
