# Proposal

## Why

OpenSea does not support Robinhood Chain's testnet (46630): MINT and Calea found no such chain on
OpenSea in the meeting of 12 October 2026. COL-7 and OPS-4 planned to prove Studio and an OpenSea
listing there. Read on 14 October: SeaDrop, Seaport 1.6 and validator V3 are deployed on 46630;
OpenSea's SignedZone is not.

## What Changes

- **COL-7**: OpenSea's handling is proven on mainnet before the drop page is published: Studio
  takes over the collection, and one team bear is sold.
- **OPS-4**: the testnet rehearsal covers everything on-chain, the whitelist through SeaDrop
  directly with an owner-set root. Studio's takeover, its tree (a test CSV checked by `compare`)
  and the sale move to mainnet before the drop page; the OpenSea testnet listing is dropped.

No requirement is added or retired, and no Scenario changes.
