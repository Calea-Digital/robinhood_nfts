# Proposal

## Why

MINT's answer at the meeting of 12 October 2026 (CQ-17): Calea holds and funds the Chainlink VRF
subscription on Arbitrum One and invoices MINT, a service Calea keeps after the handover.

## What Changes

- **RAF-8**: Calea holds the subscription. Its holder can stop the numbers but not choose an
  outcome, and can hand it to MINT keeping its id, so the draw continues.
- **OPS-1**: the subscription row names Calea.
- **OPS-5**: after the handover Calea keeps the subscription. Its Scenario's last line says so;
  the title is unchanged.
- **DEL-10**: holding and funding the subscription is a priced service.

No requirement is added or retired. OPS-5's Scenario changes its last line.
